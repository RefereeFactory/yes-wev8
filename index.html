How it works

  1. Each evening, jot down three good things that happened — however small. Noticing the good is a gentle, evidence-based way to lift your mood over time.
  2. Pick a mood and add an optional note, then press Save. Your entry appears in your journal straight away.
  3. Come back tomorrow to see your streak grow and re-read past entries with the search box on a harder day.

Your entries are saved securely in this app's permanent cloud storage, so they're waiting for you next time you visit. This is a personal wellbeing journal, not medical advice — if you're struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a qualified professional. Made with care. 💛
